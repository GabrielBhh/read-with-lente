Privacy policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Lente is an RSS reader made by Gabriel Ben Harosh Hasson. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Lente on iPhone and iPad, including its widget and its "Subscribe in Lente" and "Save to Lente" share options.

The short version

  • Lente requires no Lente account and includes no ads, third-party analytics or tracking.
  • Lente does not automatically send your subscriptions, saved articles or reading history to the developer.
  • Lente stores your subscriptions, saved-post details and reading positions on your device and in your own iCloud. Websites you visit and contact for content receive the network requests described below.
  • Lente connects directly to the websites you choose to follow. There is no Lente-operated feed or article server.

What Lente stores, and where

Lente keeps the following so it can work:

  • the sites and feeds you follow, the names you give them, and the lists you put them in;
  • downloaded posts and articles, and the ones you save;
  • where you are in each list (your "caught up" point and reading position);
  • your settings, such as theme, font and text size.

Lente stores this information on your device. When you are signed in to iCloud, your sites, lists, downloaded post details, saved-post status, caught-up points, reading positions and ‘Keep articles for’ setting can also sync through your private iCloud account. Reader settings such as theme, font and text size, and feed content caches, are kept locally. Data synced to iCloud is handled under Apple’s Privacy Policy. The developer cannot access your private iCloud data.

The widget and share options use shared storage on your device. Sites and pages saved through a share option are passed to the main app and may then sync through your iCloud account.

Connections Lente makes

Lente makes direct requests to websites you choose to follow or inspect:

  • their RSS or Atom feeds and images referenced by them;
  • their pages when finding an advertised feed, and their site icons.

The reader shows only text supplied in a feed. It does not download an article's web page to extract its body. If a feed includes a summary or no text, you can open the original in a browser. Saving an unrelated web page stores its title and link without downloading the page body.

As with any web request, those websites receive your IP address and basic technical details such as the type of device and app making the request. What they do with that is covered by their own privacy policies. Requests can also reach the image hosts or other addresses supplied by those websites. Lente does not operate a proxy for these downloads.

When you open an article or link, Lente can use Safari's built-in browser and Reader when available, or your default browser if you turn off Use in-app browser in Settings. The browser and visited website's privacy terms apply; a page may use cookies, analytics or other services of its own.

Background refresh

If Background App Refresh is on, iOS may let Lente check your feeds for new posts while you're not using it. These are the same direct connections described above.

Crash reports and beta testing

Lente includes no third-party crash-reporting or analytics tools. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and performance data with the developer, under Apple's terms. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

If you use a TestFlight beta, Apple collects and shares crash logs, usage information and any feedback you submit with the developer. Depending on how you joined the beta, this may include your name and email address. See Apple’s TestFlight privacy information.

Support requests

If you contact support, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to include, so they can respond to your request. Please avoid including sensitive information in messages or screenshots.

Your choices

  • Delete your data: delete the app, and remove Lente's data from iCloud in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage (Lente's entry may be listed by name).
  • Stop syncing: turn off iCloud for Lente in iOS Settings.
  • Take your subscriptions with you: export them as an OPML file in Lente's Settings.

Children

Lente is not directed at children and does not include advertising or tracking.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its pages use no analytics scripts or third-party fonts. GitHub receives network information, including your IP address, when serving the site; see GitHub’s Privacy Statement.
