Questions

How do I follow a site?

Tap + and type the site's address, for example example.com. Lente looks for the feed. You can also open the site in Safari, tap Share and choose "Subscribe in Lente".

How do I make a list?

On iPhone, tap the name at the top of the feed and choose New list. On iPad, choose New list in the sidebar. Give it a name and tick the sites you want in it. A site can be in more than one list. To switch lists, swipe left or right, or pick one from the same menu.

A site only shows a summary.

Lente shows the text the publisher includes in its feed. Tap Read on website to open the original article. The built-in browser uses Reader when available; some pages stay in their normal website view. You can turn off Use in-app browser in Settings to use your default browser instead.

How do I move my subscriptions from another app?

Export an OPML file from the other app, then use Import OPML in Lente's Settings. Lente can export one too.

My sites aren't showing on my iPad.

Lente syncs through iCloud. Both devices need to be signed in to the same Apple Account, with iCloud on for Lente. The first sync can take a few minutes.

A site has stopped updating.

Pull down on the feed to refresh. Sites sometimes move their feed. Check that the website itself is reachable and that Background App Refresh is enabled for Lente. If it still doesn't update, check whether the publisher lists a new feed address.
